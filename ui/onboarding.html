Welcome to HateClip

Everything you copy, one shortcut away.

How it works

HateClip lives in the menu bar and remembers what you copy. Press Cmd Shift V anywhere to open your history, type to search, and press Enter to paste the selected item into the app you were using.

Allow pasting

To paste for you, macOS needs to let HateClip send the Cmd+V keystroke. HateClip does not read your screen or what you type.
  1. Click Open System Settings.
  2. Turn on HateClip under Privacy and Security, Accessibility.
  3. Come back here. This page updates by itself.
